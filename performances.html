Performances

Throughout each semester, the Assorted Aces are honored to perform at a variety of cultural and campus-wide events, invited by student organizations and university groups. In addition to these showcases, we host our own Friends and Family Night and Choreo Days—intimate gatherings where we share our progress, celebrate growth, and perform for our teammates and supporters.

Our biggest event of the year is our Annual Spring Showcase, where we debut brand-new pieces choreographed over the course of the year. This highly anticipated event brings together hundreds of audience members—friends, family, fellow students, and alumni—with a crowd turnout of nearly 600 guests annually. Our alumni even return to perform their own pieces, making the night a celebration of both new talent and lasting connections.
