Welcome to the Assorted Aces!

Founded in 2016, Assorted Aces was created with a mission to make dance accessible to everyone—regardless of background or experience. Our goal is to empower beginners to discover their love for dance while also fostering leadership, creativity, and community among our members.

The team is structured into three levels based on skill: Clovers, Diamonds, and Hearts. Each week, Aces hosts a variety of practices for each group along with public technique classes and workshops, inviting anyone to join and grow with us. With our core belief that “anyone can dance,” most of our pieces are self-choreographed by team members, encouraging dancers to not only develop their own styles but also inspire others along the way.
