About Me

I joined the Assorted Aces Dance Team in the fall of my freshman year with no prior dance experience—just a deep curiosity and excitement to try something new. What initially drew me to Aces was the team’s commitment to teaching beginners and exploring diverse dance styles like hip hop. But what truly made me stay was the supportive, fun, and inspiring community that sparked a genuine passion in me to keep dancing and growing.

In my second year, I challenged myself to take on a larger role and give back to the team that had given me so much. I served as Performance Director on the executive board, where I helped lead group performances, coordinated practice schedules and reservations, organized setlists, and took part in planning and directing our annual spring showcase.

Now, after four years with the team, I’ve not only grown tremendously as a dancer but also as a leader and teammate. Being a part of Aces has shaped my college journey in the most rewarding ways.

If you’re interested in learning more about Assorted Aces, I invite you to explore some of our past performances and showcases. Follow us on social media and YouTube to stay updated on upcoming workshops, auditions, and events!
